# Worksheets

The seven worksheet problem sets and their full solutions are collected on this page as the course runs.
Each worksheet covers one two-lecture block and is discussed in the tutorial session for that block.

The worksheets are ungraded, and they have the same shape and level as the final written exam.
Work each problem through on paper before opening the solutions: the solutions are complete, so reading them first removes most of the benefit.

## What each worksheet covers

1. **Orbits and formation** ({ref}`lecture01`, {ref}`lecture02`): Kepler's laws, the vis-viva equation, orbital resonances, tides and the Roche limit, gravitational focusing, the mass budget of the solar nebula.
2. **Thermal evolution and differentiation** ({ref}`lecture03`, {ref}`lecture04`): cooling timescales, convective regimes, radiogenic heating, core formation, dynamo feasibility.
3. **Atmospheres** ({ref}`lecture05`, {ref}`lecture06`): scale heights and column mass, radiative equilibrium and the greenhouse effect, cloud condensation and the lifting condensation level, geostrophic winds, the faint young Sun and the carbonate-silicate thermostat.
4. **Surfaces and interiors** ({ref}`lecture07`, {ref}`lecture08`): crater chronology, impact energetics, moment of inertia, isostasy, central pressures.
5. **Terrestrial planets** ({ref}`lecture09`, {ref}`lecture10`): comparative energy balance, water loss from Venus, the Martian volatile inventory, Mercury's spin-orbit resonance.
6. **Giant planets and small bodies** ({ref}`lecture11`, {ref}`lecture12`): interior transitions, ring dynamics, isotopic dating, Kirkwood gaps, cometary activity.
7. **Exoplanets and synthesis** ({ref}`lecture13`, {ref}`lecture14`): radial-velocity and transit measurements, the habitable zone, transmission spectroscopy, the solar system in the exoplanet population.
